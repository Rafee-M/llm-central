LLM Central puts multiple AI chat websites in one small app: duck.ai, ChatGPT, Claude, DeepSeek, Z.ai and Gemini. Switch between them from the top bar quicker than browser tabs.

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li><b>Several accounts per provider</b>, each in its own container with separate logins, cookies and site data. Configure them to your liking in Settings.</li>
<li><b>Instant switching</b>: up to 10 accounts stay loaded (configurable); others reload when opened.</li>
<li><b>Block trackers</b>: the chat sites' own analytics, telemetry and fingerprinting requests are stopped inside the app.</li>
<li><b>Material You Design</b>: Express yourself with system themes, colour palettes or your wallpaper colours (Android 12+).</li>
<li><b>Assistant and sharing</b>: set the app as your digital assistant, share text or files to it, or select text anywhere and choose "Ask AI".</li>
<li><b>Prompt pre-fill</b>: shared text lands in the chosen chat's message box along with your pre-defined instructions.</li>
<li><b>Site toggles</b> for desktop mode, fullscreen, clear cache.</li>
<li><b>duck.ai extras</b>: voice chat from the assistant gesture and offline reading of your saved chats (more providers planned).</li>
<li><b>User scripts</b> per website (CSS is planned).</li>
</ul>
