LLM Central puts several AI chat websites in one small app: duck.ai, ChatGPT, Claude, DeepSeek and Z.ai. Switch between them from the top bar as quickly as between browser tabs.

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li><b>Several accounts per provider</b> (e.g. ChatGPT "Work" and "Personal"), each in its own container with separate logins, cookies and site data. Rename, hide, re-order, sign out or remove them in Settings.</li>
<li><b>Instant switching</b>: up to 3 accounts stay loaded (1–5, changeable); others reload when opened.</li>
<li><b>Assistant and sharing</b>: set the app as your digital assistant, share text or files to it, or select text anywhere and choose "Ask AI". You choose which account each of these opens.</li>
<li><b>Prompt pre-fill</b>: shared text lands in the chosen chat's message box, ready to edit and send.</li>
<li><b>Files</b>: attach files and photos exactly as they are; save files the chat sites create, with their real names, to Downloads.</li>
<li><b>Material You</b>: light, dark, black (OLED) or system theme, colour palettes or your wallpaper colours (Android 12+).</li>
<li><b>Desktop site</b> toggle per account, sign-in pop-ups, reload page, hide the status bar.</li>
<li><b>duck.ai extras</b>: voice chat from the assistant gesture and offline reading of your saved chats.</li>
<li><b>User scripts</b> per website (off by default, with a security warning).</li>
<li><b>Clear cache</b> in Settings; logins stay.</li>
</ul>

<b>Privacy</b>: no ads, no analytics, no tracking. The app only connects to the chat websites you open. External links open in your browser.

The chat services are run by their providers under their own terms; you need an account with each (except duck.ai). "Sign in with Google" doesn't work inside apps (Google blocks it), so use email login. LLM Central is not affiliated with or endorsed by any of these providers; their names and logos are trademarks of their owners.

LLM Central is a fork of duckAssist by diekaiju, itself a fork of gptAssist by woheller69.
