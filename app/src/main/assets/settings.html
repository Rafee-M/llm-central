Manage accounts

Each account keeps its own logins and site data, separate from all others.

Several accounts per provider need a newer “Android System WebView”. Update it from the Play Store.

Re-order accounts

Hold the handle on the right and drag an account up or down. Only accounts shown in the menu are listed; one you add or show again goes to the bottom.
